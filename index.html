A Love That Never Feels Old

A Personal Reflection on Faith, Hope, and God's Goodness

Introduction

I never get bored when talking about the goodness of God. Whenever I hear about it or talk about it, it always feels new to me, even though I have heard about it many times before. It is a phenomenal experience, something I have never experienced before in my life. The happiness, love, and peace that come from God are truly different, and they are things that words cannot fully explain.

Before I Knew Him

When I was younger, especially during my teenage years, which some people call a rebellious stage, I experienced sadness and a heaviness that I could not explain. I had many doubts about life, and sometimes I could not understand what I was feeling or why I was feeling that way. There were moments when I felt confused and unsure about many things.

But everything started to change when I came to know God personally. Through Him, I experienced hope, joy, and a kind of love that I had never known before. It was not simply about hearing stories about God or knowing about Him through other people. It was about personally experiencing His goodness in my own life.

How Knowing God Changed Me

Knowing God opened my eyes to reality. It helped me develop wisdom and understand that not every situation in life will be easy. I also began to understand that life has a purpose and that I do not have to face every challenge without hope.

As I continued growing in my faith, I became more grateful, hopeful, and content. I learned that not every prayer is answered in the way or at the time we expect. Sometimes the answer is yes, sometimes it is no, and sometimes we are asked to wait. Through these experiences, I learned the importance of patience and trusting God's timing.

I also realized that ang buhay ay hindi puro rosas. Life is not always filled with happiness and beautiful moments. There will be challenges, disappointments, and situations that we may not immediately understand. However, knowing God has taught me that difficulties do not mean we have to lose hope. Instead, they can become opportunities to grow in faith and learn to trust Him more.

His Goodness Never Gets Old

Until now, I am still learning and growing in my relationship with God. I know that I still have many things to understand, but one thing I can say is that knowing Him has changed the way I see life. He has taught me to become more patient, grateful, hopeful, and content.

That is why I never get tired of talking about His goodness. No matter how many times I hear about it, it never loses its meaning. Every time I remember what He has done in my life, it feels new all over again.

For me, God's goodness is more than something I simply hear about. It is something I have personally experienced, and it continues to remind me of His love, faithfulness, and grace.

His goodness is something I will never get tired of knowing, experiencing, and sharing.

Conclusion

God's goodness is something that never loses its meaning. Through every experience, I continue to learn about His love, grace, and faithfulness. Life may not always be easy, but knowing Him reminds me that there is always hope.

Thank you for taking the time to read my personal reflection about the goodness of God.
